# Erode Mandi Price Volatility & Market Opportunity Dashboard

A Power BI mini project analyzing **Erode mandi price data (2021–2025)** to identify **price trends, volatility, premium markets, and better selling opportunities**. 

## Project Overview
This dashboard transforms raw agricultural market data into actionable insights for comparing commodities, markets, and seasonal price movement. It was built using **Excel, Power Query, Power BI, and DAX**.
## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query
- Excel
- DAX

## Key Features
- Interactive slicers for commodity, market, year, price band, and spread category
- Executive overview of price levels and market coverage
- Volatility analysis using price spread
- Market opportunity analysis using price, spread, and recent movement
- Monthly, quarterly, MoM, and YoY analysis

## Key Insights

### Market coverage and baseline
- The analysis covers **104 commodities across 26 markets**, with an **average modal price of about ₹4.82K and an average price spread of about ₹650**.
- This gives a broad but moderately volatile pricing environment, so users should treat spread alongside price when making selling decisions in the next crop/market cycle.

### Seasonal price timing
- Monthly prices stay fairly stable from **January to May**, then fall sharply in **June to about ₹3.62K**, before **recovering above ₹5.15K in August and October**.
- This indicates that timing matters, so farmers and traders should avoid weaker price months like June and target stronger months in the next selling season where possible.

### Premium commodities
- **Cashewnuts (~₹61.4K), Jasmine (~₹53.96K), and Kakada (~₹42.93K)** lead average modal price by a wide margin over the next tier of commodities.
- Sellers should prioritize these premium commodities in the next opportunity cycle, but only after checking their spread and stability.

### Volatility risk
- **Dry Chillies** has the highest average price spread at about ₹12.69K, while **Moolanur** is the most volatile market at about ₹6.05K spread.
- This means high price does not automatically mean low risk, so traders should combine price, spread, and recent movement when selecting commodities and markets in the next trading cycle.

### Opportunity signal
- The dashboard identifies Bajra (Pearl Millet/Kambu) as the top opportunity commodity, Alangeyam as the top opportunity market, the highest MoM gain at ₹294.55, and the highest YoY gain at ₹11.21K.
- Users should monitor these opportunity signals in the next month and quarter because the strongest opportunities are where price strength, momentum, and manageable spread come together.
